---
title: "Meetings"
description: "Monthly data user group meetings in Portland and Vancouver, in person and online."
---

We meet the **third Wednesday of each month** in the evening, in Portland and Vancouver, and every meeting can also be joined online. Expect a featured talk, time to talk shop, and food when we're in person.

Since January 2026 we've run one shared schedule with the [Portland Azure Data User Group](https://www.meetup.com/portland-azure-user-group/). RSVPs live on Meetup, so join [Oregon Data Community](https://www.meetup.com/oregonsql/) and the partner group to get every notice.
