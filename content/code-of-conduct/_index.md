---

Oregon Data Community exists so that people who work with data can learn from each other. That only works if everyone can take part without being harassed, talked down to or made to feel unwelcome. This code applies to everyone: attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers and organizers.

**Where it applies:** our monthly meetings (in person and online), PNW Day of Data, speaker dinners and social events, our Meetup groups, email lists, chat and social media, and anything else we organize.

## What we expect

- **Be welcoming.** Make room for newcomers and for people whose background, experience or opinions differ from yours.
- **Be respectful.** Disagree about ideas, not people. Critique the query plan, not the person who wrote it.
- **Be patient.** Not everyone is working in their first language, and tone is easy to lose online.
- **Be considerate of the room.** Keep questions on topic, give the speaker space, and mute when you're not talking online.
- **Look out for each other.** If something looks wrong, check in with the person or tell an organizer.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind, including because of gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion or level of experience.
- Offensive or demeaning comments, jokes or imagery, in person, in slides or online.
- Unwelcome physical contact or sexual attention, stalking, following or intimidation.
- Photographing or recording someone who has asked not to be recorded.
- Sustained disruption of talks or discussions.
- Using our events or member lists for unsolicited sales or recruiting outreach. Sponsors are welcome in the spaces we set aside for them.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness anything that breaks this code, tell an organizer as soon as you can.

- **At an event:** find any organizer or volunteer in person.
- **Any time:** email [outreach@oregonsql.org](mailto:outreach@oregonsql.org). Your report goes to the organizers only.
- **If the concern involves an organizer,** or you'd rather not report to us: email the Microsoft Azure Data Tech Groups team at [ms-data@microsoft.com](mailto:ms-data@microsoft.com). Our group is part of that program.

We will treat every report seriously and keep it as confidential as we can. We won't ask you to confront anyone. If you feel unsafe, call **911** first.

## What happens next

Organizers will listen, ask what would help, and decide on action. That can range from a quiet word or a warning to removal from an event or the group, without refund where a fee applies. Where needed we will involve venue staff or the authorities. Retaliating against someone who reports a problem is itself a breach of this code.

## About this code

This code supports the [Azure Data Tech Groups Code of Conduct](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/azure-data-tech-groups/code-of-conduct) that applies to all groups in the program. Questions or suggestions: [outreach@oregonsql.org](mailto:outreach@oregonsql.org), or [open an issue](https://github.com/pnwdatacommunity/website/issues/new/choose).
