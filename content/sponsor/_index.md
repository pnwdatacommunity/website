Our audience is the people who run, build and buy for data platforms across Oregon and SW Washington: DBAs, data engineers, architects, analysts and the students about to join them.

## Ways to help

- **Host us (our biggest need).** We're bringing in-person meetings back to Vancouver. A room for about 30 people one weekday evening a month, with a screen and decent Wi-Fi, makes that happen. Hosts get introduced at every meeting they host.
- **PNW Day of Data:** tiered sponsorship with logo placement, an expo table and attendee reach. See [this year's sponsors](../day-of-data/).
- **Monthly meetings:** cover food for one evening and introduce your team at the start of the meeting.

Email [outreach@oregonsql.org](mailto:outreach@oregonsql.org) with "Sponsorship" in the subject line and we'll send this year's prospectus.
