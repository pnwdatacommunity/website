**We want your talk, especially if it's your first.** A user group is the friendliest room you'll ever present to: people who chose to spend their evening learning, and who'll tell you afterwards what landed.

## What we're looking for

- **45–60 minutes**, any level from fundamentals to internals.
- SQL Server and Azure SQL, PostgreSQL, Fabric and Power BI, data engineering, DevOps for databases, AI on real data, and the career side of all of it.
- Stories from production. "Here's what broke and what we changed" beats a feature tour every time.

## Remote speakers

Every meeting is streamed, so you can present from anywhere. Our evenings start around 6:00 PM Pacific. Local? Once we're back in the room in Vancouver, we'd love to have you there in person.

## First time?

Tell us, and we'll help you get ready.

## How to propose

Send a title, a short abstract and a line about you to [outreach@oregonsql.org](mailto:outreach@oregonsql.org), or [open a talk proposal on GitHub](https://github.com/pnwdatacommunity/website/issues/new?template=talk-proposal.yml).

If you want to speak at **PNW Day of Data**, its call for speakers runs on Sessionize each spring and is announced here and on Meetup.
