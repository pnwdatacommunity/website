title: "Meetings"
description: "Oregon Data Community's monthly data user group meetings: third Wednesday evenings, online, with in-person meetings returning to Vancouver."
---

We meet on the **third Wednesday of each month** in the evening: a featured talk, then time to talk shop. Meetings are online for now, and we're working to bring back in-person meetings in Vancouver, streamed as always for everyone else.

RSVP on [our Meetup group](https://www.meetup.com/oregonsql/). You'll also see our meetings posted in other Portland and Salem data groups on Meetup; it's the same meeting, so RSVP wherever you like.

For other data events around the region, including our friends at the [Portland Azure Data User Group](https://www.meetup.com/portland-azure-user-group/), see [Community](../community/).
