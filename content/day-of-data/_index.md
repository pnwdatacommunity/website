## What it is

A free Saturday of sessions from local and international speakers on SQL Server, Azure data, Fabric, analytics and the careers around them. Friday pre-cons offer a full day of depth for a small fee. It's been running for years as SQL Saturday Oregon & SW Washington. The global SQL Saturday network has become [Day of Data](https://dayofdata.org/), and we changed our name with it.

## Volunteer

The day runs on volunteers: registration desk, room monitors, setup and teardown. Email [outreach@oregonsql.org](mailto:outreach@oregonsql.org) with a subject line of "Volunteer".
