Oregon Data Community brings data professionals in Oregon and SW Washington together through monthly user group meetings with local and international presenters, and through PNW Day of Data, our free annual conference.

Our roots are in the SQL Server community, which is why you'll still see **oregonsql** in our Meetup URL and email address. The scope has widened with the job.

Everything here is run by volunteers.
